Who I Am

I am a systems architect and independent researcher. I design and build multi-layer, cross-platform software ecosystems, conduct scientific research and develop applied software - from foundational concepts to fully working systems.

As the founder of Smart Legion Lab, I am responsible for all areas of the lab's activity: scientific research, systems architecture, ecosystem design, development and publication of results.

Python is my primary development language. I also design and write in Go, C#, Kotlin, JavaScript and Rust. I implement and maintain consistent system logic across my entire multi-language stack.

My experience covers public and internal projects, open-source and closed-source development, products for individual users and solutions for businesses. My libraries and applications are used by developers worldwide, and my publications on Zenodo have a confirmed interest from the academic community.

What I Do

  • Original Paradigms (R&D) - conceptual models in computer security, information theory, and computational complexity. Every paradigm is backed by a registered DOI, a publication on Zenodo, and a working reference implementation.
  • Cross-Platform Ecosystems - architectural stacks covering cryptography, passwords and 2FA, heuristic optimization, deterministic generators, repository management, and research tasks.
  • Cores and Libraries - platform-agnostic cores, cross-language libraries, and internal frameworks implementing the core logic of systems.
  • Applications and Services - desktop GUIs, mobile clients, web services, CLI tools, and bots. User-facing, corporate, and internal.
  • Information Systems - CRM, internal platforms, business process automation.
  • Open-Source Libraries - packages (BSD-3-Clause) in international PyPI and Crates registries, used by developers worldwide.

Scientific Research & Author's Paradigms

I develop novel conceptual models aimed at replacing traditional vulnerabilities of data transmission and storage with deterministic logic, structural-statistical analysis and algorithmic approaches.

  • Local Data Regeneration Paradigm - data transmission → synchronous state discovery. DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.17264327
  • Pointer-Based Security Paradigm - data protection → data non-existence. DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.17204738
  • Position-Candidate-Hypothesis (PCH) Paradigm - structural-statistical approach to NP-complete problems. DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.17614888
  • Deterministic Game Engine - practical validation of theoretical paradigms. DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.17383447
