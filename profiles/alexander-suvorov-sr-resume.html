About Me

Systems architect, independent researcher and neural-network engineer. I design and build multi-layer, cross-platform software ecosystems - from foundational paradigms with registered DOIs to fully working systems, and I implement real neural networks from scratch (multilayer perceptrons) that train and run live in the browser. Founder of Smart Legion Lab, where I lead the full cycle: scientific research, systems architecture, ecosystem design, development and publication of results.

Primary language: Python; also design and write in Rust, Go, C#, Kotlin, JavaScript. I keep consistent logic across the whole multi-language stack. My open-source libraries (BSD-3-Clause) are used worldwide, and my Zenodo publications have confirmed interest from the academic community. I also build information systems - CRM, internal platforms and business-process automation. Open for architecture consulting, research collaborations, full-cycle development and ML/neural prototyping.
