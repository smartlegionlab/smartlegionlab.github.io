How to Play
  1. Stack falling pieces and complete lines to earn points.
  2. Use the ghost piece to see where the piece will land.
  3. Hold (C / ⇄) stores a piece for later — one swap per drop.
  4. Score grows with level; every 10 lines the speed increases.
  5. Keyboard: ← → move, ↑/X rotate, Z rotate left, ↓ soft drop, Space hard drop, C hold, P pause.
  6. On your phone: on-screen buttons — row 1: move/rotate, row 2: drop/hold, row 3: pause. Hold to repeat.
About
Tetris
version 1.0.0

Tetris is a classic block-stacking puzzle rendered on an HTML canvas with pure JavaScript.

Randomizer: 7-bag — each bag contains all seven tetrominoes, so pieces are fair.

Features: ghost piece, hold (one swap per drop), wall kicks, soft and hard drop, levels that speed up every 10 lines, local high score (localStorage).

Controls: keyboard (see Help) or on-screen buttons on touch devices.

Everything is real: gravity, collision, line clearing and scoring are simulated in pure JavaScript on an HTML canvas.
