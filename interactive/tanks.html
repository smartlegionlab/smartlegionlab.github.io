How to Play
  1. Choose a level and 1 or 2 players.
  2. Destroy all enemy tanks before your base (the eagle) is destroyed.
  3. P1: WASD + Space/F to fire · P2: arrows + L.
  4. P - pause, M - sound, Enter - start.
  5. On your phone: on-screen buttons — move on the left, fire on the right.
  6. Bonuses: ★ power, L shield, M grenade, O extra life.
About
Tanks
version 1.0.0

Tanks is a retro Battle City-style tank game with 35 original levels, 4 enemy types (basic, fast, power, armor), 4 power-ups and 1–2 player co-op.

Field: 13×13 tile grid. Brick (breaks), steel (needs power shot), water (blocks tanks), forest (hides tanks).

Goals: clear every enemy on every level while defending your base. Survive all 35 levels to win.

Controls: P1 - WASD + Space/F, P2 - arrows + L. On touch devices use the on-screen pad.

Everything is real: movement, collision, bullets and particles are simulated in pure JavaScript on an HTML canvas.
